# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Joel is the first user: a consistent journaler with years of entries, reading notes, concepts, relationship notes, and personal writing. The primary audience is serious personal journalers who want to write regularly and understand themselves across time. Broader self-reflection and structured personal-growth users may follow later.

Users usually arrive to write today's entry without friction. Once enough source material exists, they should be able to revisit their own words, ask grounded questions, and understand recurring emotional, relational, and intellectual patterns.

## Product Purpose

InnerScript is a private diary and AI memory companion. It turns personal writing into source-backed self-understanding while keeping writing, ownership, export, and privacy central.

The first success is a dependable daily loop: write, save, reopen, edit, delete, export, and receive one reflection question grounded in the current entry. Deeper search and analysis become useful only after sufficient context exists.

## Positioning

InnerScript helps people understand themselves through AI reflection grounded in their own long-term writing, not generic advice or unsupported psychological claims.

## Brand Personality

Private, reflective, grounded. The voice should feel calm, emotionally serious, honest, and careful. It may challenge the user when evidence supports that challenge, but it must preserve trust and never pretend to diagnose or replace professional care.

## Anti-references

InnerScript must not resemble a generic notes app, productivity dashboard, shallow AI chat wrapper, social product, or clinical diagnosis tool. It should avoid noisy controls, premature analytics, decorative AI spectacle, unsupported certainty, and interfaces that interrupt the writing state.

## Design Principles

1. **Writing comes first.** Today's entry is the primary daily workflow; controls and metadata stay out of the writing path.
2. **Earn every insight.** Reflection and analysis must match the amount of available context and remain traceable to the user's words.
3. **Protect emotional trust.** Saving, privacy, export, deletion, and clear AI boundaries must feel dependable.
4. **Depth arrives progressively.** Start with one grounded reflection question, then unlock broader patterns only when the source history supports them.
5. **Keep the tool quiet.** Familiar interaction patterns, restrained visual hierarchy, and purposeful state feedback should let the interface disappear into reflection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states, screen-reader names and structure, sufficient color contrast, 200% zoom and responsive reflow, reduced-motion preferences, and interactions that do not depend on color alone. Reflection and error language should stay clear, non-judgmental, and safe for emotionally sensitive material.
